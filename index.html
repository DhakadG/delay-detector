Delay Detector

Measures how far behind your Bluetooth or wired output really is, by playing a sweep and listening to it come back. Gives you the exact number to paste into VLC, mpv, or Plex.

1 · Microphone

Use your laptop's built-in mic, and keep it selected for the whole session. If you let it switch to a Bluetooth headset mic, the link drops into hands-free mode and every number becomes meaningless.

2 · Level check

3 · Reference

Play through your built-in or wired speaker and measure it. Everything after this is compared against it, which is what cancels out the microphone's own latency.
